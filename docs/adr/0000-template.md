# ADR-0000 — Template

- **Status:** template
- **Date:** —

## Context

What forced a decision. Facts, not intentions.

## Decision

What was decided, in one sentence, in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now forbidden.

## Alternatives considered

Why the obvious alternative was not chosen.

---

ADRs are the raw material for the public posts. Write one for every non-obvious
decision, at the moment it is taken, not afterwards.
